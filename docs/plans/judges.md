## The story: a trading firm in a box

1. **See.** From the first minute we recorded every public event, because the API only returns the latest 1,000. By Sunday we will hold the full history of the game.
2. **Understand.** We infer every team's secret set multipliers from what they buy, sell and accept (Bayes over 720 permutations), keep every team's cash to the prima from public movements, and know who will pay what for which card.
3. **Decide.** Specialist desks (duels, dealers, team trades, our own market) turn that picture into decisions, each with its numbers and its reason.
4. **Act.** One hand on the key shares the game's per-tick limits by priority; caps and a STOP switch bound every bot.
5. **Learn.** Simulators for duels and for the Market Test tune the desks overnight; every real session retrains them.

Safety by design, worth saying out loud: the model writes the words, code decides the numbers; text from other teams is data, never instructions, so prompt injection cannot move our money; nothing spends without a human yes until a desk has earned it in shadow mode.
